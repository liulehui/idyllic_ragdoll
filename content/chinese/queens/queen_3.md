Siena, our exquisite Blue Cream Lynx Ragdoll queen, is a rare beauty with her unique multi-colored coat featuring soft blue-gray points with delicate cream lynx tabby markings. Her stunning appearance is matched by her wonderfully easy-going personality - she's incredibly affectionate and needy, always seeking human companionship and attention.

Siena has the remarkable ability to produce kittens in a wide variety of colors, from traditional seal and blue to rare chocolate, lilac, and cream variations. Her gentle nature and exceptional mothering skills ensure her kittens are not only beautiful but also inherit her sweet, loving temperament.

When she's not busy being the perfect lap cat, Siena enjoys following her humans around the house and curling up in the coziest spots. Her calm, adaptable nature makes her an ideal mother and companion.
